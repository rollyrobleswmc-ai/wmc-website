Why WMC

Not just a course.
It’s a transformation.

WMC programs are designed to change how financial advisors think, speak, and practice — equipping them to serve clients with a level of competence, confidence, and credentialing that sets them apart.

01

Competence

WMC programs go beyond product knowledge. You will master the frameworks for comprehensive wealth planning — risk management, investment strategy, and estate conservation — so every client engagement is grounded in genuine expertise.

02

Confidence

Knowledge builds confidence. WMC graduates consistently report a shift in how they show up for clients — from cautious generalists to authoritative advisors who can discuss complex financial needs with clarity and conviction.

03

Credentials

WMC designations — CWP®, CRPP®, CIPP®, and CEPP® — are recognized by the Insurance Institute for Asia & Pacific (IIAP). These are credentials your clients and peers recognize as hallmarks of professional competence.

Our Purpose

Helping Filipinos Live Well Today and Thrive Tomorrow.

We believe financial well-being is achievable for every Filipino family — when they are guided by competent, credentialed, and compassionate financial professionals.

Our Vision

We believe in the vision of financial freedom for Filipinos. This can happen if families possess the knowledge, skills and tools needed for sound financial stewardship and commit to execute carefully crafted financial plans.

Our Mission

Anchored on Divine Guidance, we contribute to nation building as we empower Filipinos to be effective stewards of their wealth through relevant research, timely information and life-changing education consistent with global standards.
