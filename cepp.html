About the Course

The Certified Estate Planner,
Philippines Designation

Estate Planning requires a good working knowledge of Philippine laws to find useful applications in local settings. It includes transfer tax management as well as lawful distribution of assets to heirs.

The Estate Planning Course is designed to provide participants with practice-based knowledge and skills that will equip them to be competent guides in the process of estate planning.

Earn the designation Certified Estate Planner, Philippines (CEPP®) — highly regarded in wealth management circles as the standard of practice, competence and excellence for professionals whose mandate centers primarily on the orderly transfer and succession of intergenerational wealth.

Key Benefits

Comprehensive understanding of Philippine Laws pertaining to transfer of wealth

Learn strategies and tools to diminish adverse effects of transfer taxes

Understand the lawful distribution of wealth to heirs

Identify common issues encountered in estate planning and how to resolve them

Acquire the Certified Estate Planner, Philippines (CEPP®) designation

The Expert Who Teaches It

Course Leader

Atty. Angelo M. Cabrera

Chief Knowledge Officer, Estate Planning

Atty. Angelo M. Cabrera is the author of “Thy Will Be Done” — one of the most authoritative books on estate planning in the Philippines. He is a sought-after resource person in estate planning forums, known for his ability to simplify complex legal statutes into easily understood, practically applicable guidance.

His wit, humor, and true stories enrich his lectures while providing his audience with a wide array of practical solutions drawn from years of experience and thorough understanding of the nuances of estate conservation.

Participants consistently leave with a better appreciation of succession and inheritance issues, including the instruments used in proper estate transfer. Atty. Cabrera is also the Founder and Chairman Emeritus of the Wealth Management Center.
