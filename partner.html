In March 2014, Wealth Management Center partnered with the Insurance Institute for Asia and the Pacific, Inc. (IIAP) to administer the examinations for all those who want to earn the Certified Wealth Planner and Certified Estate Planner, Philippines designations.

This partnership means that when a WMC graduate displays their designation — on a business card, a LinkedIn profile, or in a client presentation — it carries the weight of regional institutional recognition that peers and clients trust.

About IIAP

Established 1974

Founding

Established on August 7, 1974 by the signing of Presidential Decree No. 530, IIAP is a non-stock, non-profit corporation that primarily provides educational training programs in insurance in the Asia Pacific region.

Regional Standing

Formerly known as the Asian Institute of Insurance, IIAP is one of four centers appointed in April 1979 at the Round Table Meeting in Bangkok, Thailand to elevate the field of insurance and reinsurance to higher standards, particularly among developing countries.

With its mission to promote the growth of the insurance industry, IIAP envisions to be the primary and preferred institute for insurance in the Asia–Pacific region.
