A Message of Welcome

Welcome to WMC

It is my pleasure to welcome you to the Wealth Management Center (WMC). We at WMC are proud to tell you of who we are, what we stand for and why we should trek the same path in your journey to a better future.

We believe in the vision of a better, prouder and more progressive Philippines. We share in the belief that for that vision to become reality, every Filipino must commit to the pursuit of financial viability and sustainability as his contribution to the nation. This revolution from the grassroots cannot take place if families do not possess the knowledge, skills and tools needed to lead profitable lives.

This is the essence of what we do in WMC. We train, empower and certify finance professionals from diverse industries through knowledge and education to provide the Filipino population with skilled and compassionate guides in their journey to a better life. Our programs adhere to the highest standards of international practice and are administered by industry veterans whose experience and expertise provide immeasurable value to both our students and their clients.

We at WMC subscribe to a purpose that is consistent with our belief in a Higher Authority. This ideal in turn guides us in the manner and conduct of our work. As in a prism, we stand at the crossroads of our nation's history with our offering in hand. Already, we glimpse a vision of a glorious future yet we keep a wary, watchful eye on a storied, oftentimes tumultuous past.

Our dream is to undertake the crucial task of training and developing a sufficient number of finance professionals who will consequently navigate and lead a significant portion of the Filipino nation in the collective endeavor of growing the country through personal wealth building.

We are confident that with enough of our countrymen finding common cause with our vision, we will see the re-emergence of the Philippines as the Pearl of the Orient, a distinction we once so richly deserved. We invite you to join us as we help usher in a new era of abundance and grace for future generations of Filipinos whose lives will be better by our having been here.

Welcome!

Atty. Angelo M. Cabrera
Founder & Chairman Emeritus, Wealth Management Center

About the Founder

Atty. Angelo M. Cabrera

Atty. Angelo M. Cabrera is one of our country's renowned estate planning experts and a sought-after speaker for estate planning forums nationwide. After finishing his law studies at the University of San Carlos in Cebu and passing the bar, he set up his private law practice focused on helping clients with proper estate transfer.

His strength lies in his ability to communicate the many and oftentimes complicated legal concepts in a manner that is easily understood. Prior to his law practice, Atty. Cabrera was a senior executive of one of the largest financial institutions in the country.

Education

  • High School — Ateneo de Manila
  • B.S. Economics — University of the Philippines, Diliman
