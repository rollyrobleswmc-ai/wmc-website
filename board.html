Leadership

Board of Directors

The accomplished leaders who guide WMC's mission, strategic direction, and uncompromising standards of excellence.

Wealth Management Center is guided by a board of accomplished leaders in the Philippine financial services industry — seasoned professionals whose collective expertise spans wealth management, insurance, investment, law, and estate planning. Anchored on WMC's vision of a financially empowered Filipino people and its mission of contributing to nation building through life-changing education, the Board steers WMC's strategic direction with the same standard of excellence it expects from every graduate — competent, trustworthy, and committed to the greater good.

Founder & Chairman Emeritus

Atty. Angelo M. Cabrera

Atty. Cabrera is one of the Philippines' foremost estate planning experts and a sought-after speaker on wealth transfer, succession, and the legal dimensions of financial planning. He is the author of the best-selling book “Thy Will Be Done — Understanding the What, Why & When of Estate Planning.”
