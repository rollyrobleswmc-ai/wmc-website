Management Seminar

Covering the fundamentals of trust and investment management — with relevant, substantial content on the rigors of trust operations and investment programs. Designed for professionals in banking, investment houses, insurance companies, and other financial and asset management institutions.

About the Course

The Certified Trust Professional
Designation

Completion of this course accelerates compliance with the requirements of the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) for trust officers to undergo a 90-hour training program.

Graduates earn the Certified Trust Professional (CTP) designation. This seminar is open to participants from the banking sector, investment houses, insurance companies and other financial and asset management institutions.

Key Benefits

Comprehensive understanding of trust operations and investment principles

Learn from trust and investment professionals with high technical competence and decades of experience

Accelerated compliance with BSP’s required 90-hour training program for Trust Officers

Eligibility to work in the Trust Operations of banks

Earn the Certified Trust Professional (CTP) designation

How It Works

Course Methodology

Lecture & Discussion

In-depth instruction from industry practitioners with decades of real-world experience in banking, trust, and investment management.

Breakout Rooms

Collaborative case analysis and discussion sessions designed to reinforce learning through practical application.

Assessments

Learning Checks are given every two weeks. A Final Exam is administered at the conclusion of the course to test comprehension and retention.
