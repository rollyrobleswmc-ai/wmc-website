About the Course

Wealth Creation. Wealth Protection. Wealth Conservation.

This course equips participants with the knowledge and skills to competently guide clients in crafting holistic and comprehensive plans in the three important areas of wealth management: wealth creation, wealth protection, and wealth conservation.

The three modules are Insurance Planning, Investment Planning, and Estate Planning, each conducted by a Course Leader with decades of experience in the financial services industry.

IIAP-Recognized Designations Earned

Graduates earn the Certified Wealth Planner (CWP®), Certified Risk Planner, Philippines (CRPP®), Certified Investment Planner, Philippines (CIPP®), and Certified Estate Planner, Philippines (CEPP®). The Insurance Institute for Asia & Pacific (IIAP) considers these as hallmarks of competence in wealth management and financial planning.
